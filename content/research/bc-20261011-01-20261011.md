### B. She did not know Zoe was the Manhunter until the episode-four script

3. Vulture, Roxana Hadadi, 13 September 2026, https://www.vulture.com/article/poorna-jagannathan-lanterns-manhunter-interview.html — spoiler line on the page: “Spoilers for Lanterns through episode five, ‘Lights Out,’ which premiered on HBO on Sunday, September 13.”

Reporter sentence, not her quote:

> Jagannathan had no idea she’d been cast as the show’s big bad until she sat down to read the script for episode four, “The Weenie” — when she learned Zoe faked her own death to throw off Hal and John from realizing she’s the Manhunter, Jagannathan thought she took the wrong dose of a weed gummy.

Her words on that read, as printed:

> I got the gig — you’d think it would be a great opportunity to tell me that. It didn’t happen. I was speaking to co-creator and showrunner Chris Mundy all the time to figure things out, then I got episode four, where I “die,” and I just was like, That’s interesting and abrupt, but I’m still so happy and honored to be part of the DC Universe. Because I don’t know the genre, I was like, Maybe this abruptness is just part of what happens? People just blow up? In Urdu, the word is khodafez, which is bye-bye.

Interviewer’s next line, not hers: “In Farsi, too. Khodafez — it was a major ‘bye!’ moment.”

She continues:

> I come to the end of the script. It’s pretty late. I have popped a gummy by then — and I read that I’m the Manhunter. I swear to God, I think I’m not reading properly. It was like, What did I just ingest? [Laughs] I was shocked they didn’t tell me. I was shocked I was playing such a pivotal role. When the first khodafez happened [when Zoe fakes her own death in “The Weenie”], it felt in line with what happens to women of color — an early exit. I was really taken with my lack of imagination, because on the internet, everyone has my number by now. I did not see it coming.

The bracket is Vulture’s. The photo caption repeats: “I was really taken with my lack of imagination, because on the internet, everyone has my number by now. I did not see it coming.”
