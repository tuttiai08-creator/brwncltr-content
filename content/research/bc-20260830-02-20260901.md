## Working topic

    - **Do not put “23-year-old” in the reporter’s voice as biography.** Attribute it as the film’s logline.

20. **Mileage / transport conflict:**
    - Film/marketing: **6,000 miles** by bicycle.
    - CNN: **3,600 km** (about 2,200 miles) “bicycle journey”; “on others he admits taking many **lifts** — even being gifted a **train ticket**.” Photo caption: Istanbul-to-Vienna train ticket gifted.
    - BBC: ~**70 km (44 miles)** a day; reached Europe 28 May via Istanbul and Vienna, then **travelled to Gothenburg by train**. Does not print a 6,000-mile total. Exact closing quote (re-fetched 2026-09-01, same URL): “I did what I had to, I had no money but I had to meet her. I was cycling for love, but never loved cycling. It's simple.” BBC also prints that he still doesn’t understand “why people think it was a big deal to cycle to Europe.”
    - BBC Konark (same URL, attributed color only): “I became emotional when PK showed me the Konark. I had this image of the temple stone wheel framed in my student room back in London, but I had no idea where this place actually was. And here I was standing in front of it.”
    - Nat Geo headline: “more than **4,000 miles**.” PK: “I didn’t just travel by bicycle. I got **rides with trucks**.”
    - PK’s own site (pkmahanandia.com, fetched via search 2026-09-01): “**7,000-mile**” journey — marketing/memoir language; do not elevate over BBC/CNN.
    - **Surface the conflict. Do not pick 6,000 vs 3,600 km vs 4,000 vs 7,000 in the lede.**

21. **Meeting year:** BBC/CNN/Nat Geo: **1975** (Nat Geo: 17 Dec 1975). Moveable Fest: **1976**. Use BBC/CNN/Nat Geo; flag Moveable Fest as a conflict, do not split the difference.

25. BBC video title frames **caste inequality** as part of what Chopra Jonas is talking about. That is a FACT about the BBC’s framing, not proof the film “is about” caste in a legal sense.
