## 8. Rule 10 angle candidates

What the big outlets ran: BBC, Billboard Canada, Pollstar, Indian Express and India Today all ran Wembley as "history" and "first Punjabi". Indian outlets separately ran the Satluj takedown and the Indore/Ahmedabad song curbs. **No fetched outlet connects the two**: the uncensored London stadium night, then the India homecoming where officials police the setlist. BBC's Wembley piece doesn't mention *Satluj*. No source found shows him mentioning *Satluj* or Khalra at Wembley (search r 2026-11-17).

**A. "The songs London sang, India tried to ban." (PICK)**
- Spine (INTERPRETATION): the diaspora stadium is where Diljit's whole show runs uncut. At home, two months later, the same catalog ("Patiala Peg", "Case") comes with police conditions, and he sang "Patiala Peg" in Indore anyway.
- Backing FACTS:
  - Wembley setlist incl. "Patiala Peg" and "Case" (Noise Melt, single source, needs a second source).
  - Fan flags banned "as requested by the tour" (Wembley).
  - Indore four-song bar (India Today) and "Patiala Peg" performed anyway (Billboard India).
  - Ahmedabad notice (Indian Express).
  - "Nobody can stop me from coming here" and "Yeh nahi gaana, woh nahi karna" (Billboard India).
  - "India mein stadium ho, aur Punjabi udhar na ho, yeh toh phir ho nahi sakta" (NDTV), announced from the Wembley stage.
- Why: it's a diaspora-vs-home thread none of the big outlets ran. It has a person in a room (Indore, or the Wembley announcement) and his own stage lines. It's live because Ahmedabad is Sat 21 Nov, so the piece can go up before or right after the show where the notice applies.
- Risk: "Patiala Peg"/"Case" at Wembley needs a second source (fan video counts only as social evidence). Don't imply the government banned the songs nationally. These were district child-protection requests acted on by local police.

**B. "Satluj: the film the diaspora couldn't watch either."**
- Spine (INTERPRETATION): in 2025 *Sardaar Ji 3* skipped India and played only overseas, so the diaspora was the outlet. In 2026 *Satluj*, his Jaswant Singh Khalra film, was pulled in India and then from ZEE5 worldwide, so the overseas release valve closed too.
