## Working topic

Asha Bhosle and Anoushka Shankar on Gorillaz' ninth studio album *The Mountain* (Kong), nominated 16 November 2026 for Best Alternative Music Album at the 69th Grammy Awards. Starting URL supplied by the owner: https://www.grammy.com/albums/the-mountain-2/

## Central editorial question

What did each woman actually do on the record, in the room, and on a stage, and what does the 16 November nomination line still refuse to say?

If a draft is only "India had two Grammy stories this week" or "Gorillaz used Indian guests," it should not proceed.

## Labeling key

- **FACT:** what a named page actually says.
- **INTERPRETATION:** a reading. Not for the lede as established fact.
- **UNRESOLVED:** not checked, or checked and still open.
- **SOURCE CONFLICT:** named pages disagree. Do not pick a winner in the lede.

## Source tiers

- **T1** — Recording Academy pages; Gorillaz' own store; a label or distributor release; an official broadcaster or official-channel title.
- **T2** — a named interview or obituary whose wording was fetched this run.
- **T3** — a news rewrite used to show what the public version says, or to corroborate a quote whose original post was not opened.
- **Not used as evidence** — Wikipedia. The discovery radar of 17 November 2026. Unfetched search snippets.

## Listening

This run did not play the album. No independent description of the mix, the vocal, or the sitar belongs in a draft. Sonic lines below are attributed.

## What the discovery note got wrong

The 17 November radar (unverified, not a source) said Anoushka Shankar was on multiple tracks, Asha Bhosle on "The Shadowy Light," and "Ajay Prasanna and Amaan and Ayaan Ali Khan" among the guests, with the angle that Bhosle was "still" on a nominated cartoon-band record.

Checked result: the track billing is real; the surname on the record is **Bangash**, not Ali Khan; Bhosle died on 12 April 2026, so "still" is the wrong tense; the Grammy nominee is **Gorillaz**, not either woman.
