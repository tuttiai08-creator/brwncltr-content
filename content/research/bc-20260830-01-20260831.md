## Verified factual findings

Each item is what a named page actually says. Boxscore and Live Nation figures are **as reported**, not independently audited here.

1. **Aura World Tour Boxscore (cumulative, as of Billboard Pro fetch):** $45 million gross; 317,000 tickets. Source: Billboard Pro, “Diljit Dosanjh's Aura World Tour Nears $50M Gross,” https://www.billboard.com/pro/diljit-dosanjh-aura-world-tour-gross/ — fetched 2026-08-31. Publish date not printed on the fetched page. Billboard author index associates Eric Frankenberg with “Diljit Dosanjh Nears $50M Gross on Record-Breaking Aura World Tour” (https://www.billboard.com/author/eric-frankenberg/).

2. **North American leg:** more than 225,000 fans; $32 million; 15 shows; biggest NA leg by a Punjabi artist in Boxscore history (Billboard Pro). Live Nation’s Nidhish Varughese, quoted on that page: “With this North American leg, he broke his own record, drawing more than 225,000 fans and grossing $32 million across 15 shows, surpassing his record-breaking 2024 Dil-Luminati Tour, which previously stood as the highest grossing North American tour by a South Asian artist.” Same quote cluster in MBW 25 Jun 2026 (Live Nation, not Boxscore) and CelebrityAccess 30 Jun 2026.

3. **U.S. vs Canada split (Billboard Pro):** 10 U.S. shows, eight markets; five Canada shows. “While the U.S. covered the lion’s share of the North American leg’s earnings, Dosanjh’s five shows in Canada sold more tickets.” Rogers Centre 31 May: $5.9 million (peak revenue). BC Place: 42,700 tickets (peak attendance). MSG 24–25 May: $5.1 million / 26,900 tickets. Chase Center 20–21 Jun: $4.5 million / 26,800 tickets.

4. **Oceania (Billboard Pro):** $13 million; 90,100 tickets; five Australia + one New Zealand; stadiums Melbourne and Sydney. MBW attributes “first stadium headline sell-outs by a Punjabi artist in Australia” to Live Nation.

5. **2024 Dil-Luminati NA (MBW 25 Jun 2026, Live Nation):** 215,000 fans; $27 million. Billboard Pro does not reprint the $27 million figure on the fetched page; Varughese only says Aura surpassed Dil-Luminati. Use $27 million only with MBW/Live Nation attribution, not as Boxscore.

6. **Wembley date:** Saturday 12 September 2026. Confirmed on: Wembley Stadium event page; Wembley events index; artist tour page; Billboard Pro; Billboard Canada 1 Jun 2026; Stereoboard 2 Jul 2026.

7. **Artist-site sold-out label:** diljitdosanjh.com/tour, Europe section, “Sep 12, 2026 London Wembley Stadium Sold out” — fetched 2026-08-31. Billboard Pro attributes sellout to “Dosanjh’s website,” not to Boxscore.

8. **Wembley venue copy (fetched 2026-08-31):** “Diljit Dosanjh will bring his Aura World Tour to Wembley Stadium on Saturday 12 September.” “Tickets go on general sale on Friday 12 June from 10am.” Official sellers: Ticketmaster & Wembley Stadium. Hospitality: “Don’t miss out on securing your spot at this sell-out show.” Age: no under 5s; no under 14s on pitch seating; under-14s with 18+ adult. The general-sale sentence is leftover onsale copy, not a 31 Aug inventory statement.

9. **Accessible tickets:** Wembley Support Portal article “Diljit Dosanjh - Saturday 12 September 2026”: “ALL accessible tickets have sold out for this event.” Fetched 2026-08-31. That is **accessible inventory**, not proof every ticket type is gone.

10. **Onsale mechanics:** Billboard Canada 1 Jun 2026 — artist presale 10 Jun 10:00 BST; general 12 Jun 10:00 BST via Live Nation. Matches Wembley page’s 12 Jun 10am general sale.

11. **Announcement:** 31 May 2026, Rogers Centre. Billboard Canada 1 Jun 2026 (first published Billboard U.K.). MBW 25 Jun 2026 quotes the stage line about Wembley / Jackson / Prince. Tribune India quotes a shorter line and credits Instagram @diljitdosanjh for an image (social evidence that the account posted an image, not proof of sellout).

12. **Arijit Singh / Tottenham, 5 Sep 2025 — blocks “first Indian UK stadium” for Diljit:** Tottenham Hotspur Stadium event page: “first Indian artist ever to headline a UK stadium.” BBC (Harry Low, 5 Sep 2025): “first Indian-born artist to headline a UK stadium.” Billboard Canada 1 Jun 2026 itself says Arijit “became the first Indian to headline a stadium show in the U.K. at the neighboring Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in 2025” in the same article that also calls Diljit first Indian *at Wembley*. **Do not write “first Indian stadium act in the UK.”** “First Punjabi Wembley headline” is a narrower claim (see conflicts).

13. **Europe remaining as of Billboard Pro (written when nine shows remained):** Amsterdam, Berlin, Manchester, Paris, “among other key markets,” plus London Wembley. Full nine-date list as of Stereoboard 2 Jul 2026 and Ticketmaster UK search extracts (TM full page empty on fetch): Berlin Uber Arena 21 Aug 20:00; Dublin 3Arena 25 Aug 18:30; Paris Adidas Arena 28 Aug 20:00; Vienna Wiener Stadthalle Halle D 30 Aug 19:30; Milan Unipol Forum 5 Sep; Amsterdam Ziggo Dome 7 Sep 20:00; London Wembley 12 Sep 17:00; Birmingham Utilita Arena 18 Sep 18:00; Manchester Co-op Live 20 Sep 18:30.

14. **Presale scale (Live Nation via MBW / CelebrityAccess, not Boxscore):** >130,000 tickets in first two days; “largest North American presale for a South Asian artist in Live Nation history.” Attribute to Live Nation, not Billboard Boxscore.

15. **O2 2024:** Billboard Canada and MBW: last London show Oct 2024, 20,000-capacity O2.
