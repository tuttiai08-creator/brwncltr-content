## Working topic

NFL owners unanimously approved the sale of the Seattle Seahawks from the Estate of Paul G. Allen to a group led by the Khosla family on 26 August 2026. Regular-season kickoff is Wednesday 9 September 2026 vs the New England Patriots. As of 1 September 2026 the transaction has not been reported as closed.
