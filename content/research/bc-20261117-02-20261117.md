## 5. How the song was made

**FACT, first-hand quote, trade original.** Hindustan Times, Saxena, Devan:

> In this, I have brought in fusion of Indian classical elements along with modern music. Yes, I have done the composition and music production, but Susha's voice did the magic and while I was campaigning for the song everyone had praise for her voice. I have known her for over 20 years, but the collaboration happened now.

> I wanted to record in Raag Bardiya, but she suggested doing it in Raag Des and I recorded her voice and then made everything around her singing and added my vocals and arrangements. I would love to do a Hindi version of this if I get a proper translation.

**FACT, first-hand quote.** Billboard India, Devan:

> I wanted people like me, who aren’t from a Carnatic background, to want to listen to a Carnatic song.

> She gave it a very modern feel. At the end, she keeps repeating ‘ Kanna‘ [a word of affection for the Hindu deity Krishna] with different improvisations. Till today, I cry every time I hear that section.
