## B. Commission-free, and October 2019

**FACT.** Robinhood's public trading app, as of the 12 March 2015 CNN story, let people trade stocks for free. The December 2013 TechCrunch story is the announcement of zero-commission trading and a fundraise, not the public brokerage launch. The April 2013 app was a predictions app.

**FACT.** Charles Schwab press release: beginning 7 October 2019, U.S. stock, ETF, and options online commissions go from $4.95 to zero. Options still 65 cents per contract. Charles Schwab: "Beginning October 7, every Schwab client can trade U.S. stocks, ETFs and options commission-free." https://pressroom.aboutschwab.com/press-releases/press-release/2019/In-Conjunction-With-Chuck-Schwabs-New-Book-Invested-Schwab-Removes-the-Final-Pricing-Barrier-to-Investing-Online-by-Eliminating-U.S.-Stock-ETF-and-Options-Commissions/default.aspx

**FACT.** E*TRADE, Business Wire datelined 2 October 2019, filed as an SEC exhibit: eliminates retail commissions for online U.S.-listed stock, ETF, and options trades, effective 7 October 2019. Options contract charge cut to $0.65; active-trader pricing stays $0.50 per contract. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015780/000115752319002029/a52105029ex99_1.htm

**FACT.** TD Ameritrade's own 21 October 2019 earnings release, SEC exhibit: "Effective Oct. 3, 2019, TD Ameritrade eliminated commissions for its online exchange-listed stock, ETF and option trades, moving from $6.95 to $0." Options still $0.65 per contract. A $6.95 commission still applied to OTC stocks. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1173431/000119312519270922/d822321dex991.htm

**FACT.** A Business Wire reprint carried by MarketChameleon dates the TD announcement 1 October 2019, 6:13 PM, effective Thursday 3 October 2019, $6.95 to $0, options $0.65. https://marketchameleon.com/PressReleases/i/880781/AMTD/the-best-just-got-better-td-ameritrade — reprint, not the wire page itself. The effective date matches TD's later SEC filing.

**INTERPRETATION.** October 2019 is the month the three incumbents cut the stock commission to zero. They did not do it on one day. Schwab and E*TRADE effective 7 October. TD effective 3 October. Options were not free: a per-contract fee remained. Do not write "they dropped all fees."

**UNRESOLVED.** The clock time of Schwab's announcement. The Schwab release states the 7 October effective date. A separate announcement dateline was not on the text retrieved here.

## C. Confetti, the Massachusetts complaint, the removal

**FACT.** Massachusetts Securities Division administrative complaint, Robinhood Financial, LLC, docket E-2020-0047, dated 16 December 2020. The complaint alleges gamification, defined there as applying game elements to boost engagement. It says Robinhood "rewards customers with colorful confetti raining down their screens after executing trades." Paragraph 41: "Each time a customer makes a trade on Robinhood's platform, confetti rains down on the screen." The complaint also alleges a 2019 cash-management waitlist that rewarded daily taps. These are allegations in a complaint, not a court finding. https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/securities/download/MSD-Robinhood-Financial-LLC-Complaint-E-2020-0047.pdf and the Massachusetts archives copy dated 2020-12-16, https://archives.lib.state.ma.us/entities/publication/0df91893-9a9e-4e47-b940-c9ba5b34b3df

**FACT.** CNBC, 31 March 2021, quoting a Robinhood blog post that day: "In the past, we used the same confetti design to celebrate firsts with customers. Those included customers' first trades, their first steps with cash management, and successful referrals of friends and family. Now, we're introducing new, dynamic visual experiences..." CNBC says Robinhood called the change an effort to remove a "distraction" from "democratizing investing." The blog URL itself was not opened. https://www.cnbc.com/2021/03/31/robinhood-gets-rid-of-confetti-feature-amid-scrutiny-over-gamification.html

**FACT.** AP, photo caption and story, 31 March 2021: Robinhood "will begin retiring the confetti" starting Wednesday 31 March 2021. "The last pops will go off next week," replaced by slower animations. Confetti had marked milestones such as a first deposit or upgrading to Gold. https://apnews.com/article/business-3c83ae69e7dbc4ddc825e9af308e9c78

**FACT.** Massachusetts consent order in E-2020-0047 (and a separate cybersecurity docket). Robinhood's offer of settlement is dated 17 January 2024. The order says Robinhood admits the factual allegations in Section VI(B). Those admitted facts include: "Digital confetti raining down from the top of the screen after a customer's first trade," and "Robinhood ceased use of the digital confetti feature on its Application as of March 31, 2021." The order also requires Robinhood to permanently cease confetti "or other celebratory imagery directly tied" to the described use. The order describes the case as opened by an amended complaint filed 21 October 2021. The 16 December 2020 document is the original complaint. https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/securities/download/RH-Consent-Order.pdf

**SOURCE CONFLICT.** The December 2020 complaint says confetti after every trade. The company's March 2021 blog, as quoted by CNBC, and the later admitted consent-order facts, say confetti after firsts, and the order specifies the first trade. Do not write "confetti on every trade" as settled fact. The allegation and the admission are different sentences.

**SOURCE CONFLICT.** AP on 31 March 2021 said the last pops would go off the following week. The 2024 consent order says the feature ceased as of 31 March 2021. Use the order for the date the company later agreed to. Do not pretend the animation vanished at midnight if the draft is also quoting AP.

**INTERPRETATION.** "Gamified" is the Division's word in the complaint, and it became the press word. It is fair in the piece as the charge, attributed. It is not a finding that the app was a game.
