## Full draft

It was maybe 2 AM, and Naisha was working on *so then* when Ty Dolla $ign came by the studio with Poo Bear. “He jumped on it because the energy felt right and we were having fun,” she told EE72’s Erica Rana, a day before the rest of *911* arrived.

That session is the first track on the three-song debut, billed on Apple as Naisha & Ty Dolla $ign. Apple dated the full set — *so then*, *CONTRABAND*, and *jaana* — to 7 August 2026, copyright Desi Trill Music under exclusive license to Interscope Records. Clash, filing the drop, printed the line she wanted on the record: “With ‘911’, I finally get to share a project that feels completely mine.”

On EE72 she put the famous names where she wanted them. “Skrillex is one of the executive producers on the EP alongside Dilip Venkatesh and me,” she said. “More than that, they are family to me.” She calls them Sonny and Dilip. “I have been on a lot of Sonny’s music, and he has been part of almost everything I have made too.” iTunes and the official video still bill *CONTRABAND* as Naisha with Skrillex, BEAM, and Dilip. *jaana* is billed to her alone.

The title, she told Rana, is not a novelty sticker on an American emergency number. “For me, 911 is more of a feeling than an emergency number.” “By the time this project was finished, getting it out felt like a matter of life or death. I couldn’t keep it inside anymore.” “I have always felt like a child of the world,” she said, “so it felt right to take something so globally recognised and make it personal.”

The house she describes is specific. “My mama used to sing on the radio in India when she was younger, and my grandmother made sure I learned about Hindustani classical music.” She told Rana her mom was always playing Whitney Houston, Fantasia Barrino, Asha Bhosle, Grace Jones, Bob Marley and the Wailers, ABBA; her dad loved Qawwali, so she grew up hearing Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, Guru Dutt and Geeta Dutt. “One night during Covid, I downloaded Logic because I just wanted to create. I taught myself to produce, and everything since then has happened pretty organically.”

The clip that went out with the drop is *CONTRABAND*, filmed in Mumbai. uDiscover says it was developed with creative direction from Naisha; Ruby and Colin Tilley directed, following her through city streets, crowds, and choreographed dance sequences. On MixTape Madness, talking about *so then* in July, she had already described the way she likes a record to arrive: “Even Ty becoming part of it happened naturally. He heard the song, connected with it, and wanted to be part of it. Those are the moments I live for.”

“This project is just the beginning,” she told EE72. “This is just the appetizer from my side.”
