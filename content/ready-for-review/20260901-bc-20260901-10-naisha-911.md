## Full draft

It was maybe 2 AM, and Naisha was working on *so then* when Ty Dolla $ign came by the studio with Poo Bear. “He jumped on it because the energy felt right and we were having fun,” she told EE72’s Erica Rana, a day before the rest of *911* arrived.

It’s the first of three tracks on *911*, which Desi Trill and Interscope put out on August 7, and she wanted one thing clear about it: “With ‘911’, I finally get to share a project that feels completely mine.”

Until August, most people knew her as the voice on Skrillex records, from *Talk To Me* with Four Tet to two songs on *SOMA* in June.

“My mama used to sing on the radio in India when she was younger.” Her mom played Whitney and Asha Bhosle, her dad played Nusrat, and one night during Covid she downloaded Logic “because I just wanted to create” and taught herself to produce.

That is the line that gets her to the credit that matters. “Skrillex is one of the executive producers on the EP alongside Dilip Venkatesh and me,” she said. “More than that, they are family to me.” *jaana*, the last track, is hers alone.

Ruby and Colin Tilley shot the *CONTRABAND* video in Mumbai, but she had the creative direction.

“That’s honestly how I make music,” she said of the 2 AM session. “It’s not about releases or features. It’s creating something first.”
