## Full draft

It was maybe 2 AM, and Naisha was working on *so then* when Ty Dolla $ign came by the studio with Poo Bear. "He jumped on it because the energy felt right and we were having fun," she told EE72's Erica Rana, a day before the rest of *911* arrived.

It's the first of three tracks on *911*, which Desi Trill and Interscope put out on August 7, and the first project she says "feels completely mine."

Until then she was mostly the voice on Skrillex records, *Talk To Me* in 2024 and two songs on *SOMA* this June. On *911* she's an executive producer next to him and Dilip Venkatesh, and she creatively directed the *CONTRABAND* video that Ruby and Colin Tilley shot in Mumbai.

Her mama sang on the radio in India, her grandmother made sure she learned Hindustani classical, and the house ran on Whitney, Asha Bhosle and Nusrat. Then one night during Covid she downloaded Logic "because I just wanted to create," taught herself to produce, and says everything since "has happened pretty organically."

She calls *911* "more of a feeling than an emergency number," and she kept it, American as it is, because people everywhere know it. "I have always felt like a child of the world, so it felt right to take something so globally recognised and make it personal."

Getting to music that felt like hers took a long time, she said, and by the time *911* was finished, putting it out "felt like a matter of life or death."

"It's not about releases or features," she said of that 2 AM session. "It's creating something first."
