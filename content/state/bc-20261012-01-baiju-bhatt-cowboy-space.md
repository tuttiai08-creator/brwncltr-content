## Angle

Start in the NASA Langley wind tunnels.

On The Peel (transcript retrieved 2026-11-24), Bhatt says his father was from Bhavnagar in Gujarat in the 1970s and 80s, wanted to study physics, and later got a job at NASA at Langley Air Force Base. He remembers the giant wind tunnels there from when he was a kid. He studied physics at Stanford. He calls the finance years a detour: "had a little bit of a detour in my professional career. I went into finance."

The detour was Robinhood. For millennial and Gen Z readers, Robinhood means GameStop, January 2021, the day the app restricted buying.

Now the same guy is building rockets. Cowboy Space calls the rocket Big Iron.

The spine, in order: physicist's son, Stanford physics, Robinhood co-founder, the GameStop freeze, Aetherflux, Cowboy Space and his own rocket.

Use his Peel wording for the job ("at NASA at Langley Air Force Base") and for the tunnels ("giant wind tunnels at Langley Air Force Base"). Do not silently rename the site.
