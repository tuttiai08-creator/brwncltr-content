## Proposed outline

Four beats. Not article prose. Each caveat once. Sourcing stays in Verification.

1. **Open on the name.** No date in the lede. Take in the first breath: Baiju Bhatt, the Robinhood co-founder, named the rocket company Cowboy, and the upper stage is supposed to stay in orbit as a 1-megawatt data center. Best line up top, what he told Payload: the name "captures the scale of ambition that we've got with the new set of objectives." Mission line next to it if it earns the space: "Powering humanity from the high frontier." One hedge, here only: none of it has flown. Don't open on Poquoson, a net-worth figure, or an identity label.

2. **The bet.** Cowboy Space Corporation, the company that was Aetherflux. $275 million Series B at a $2 billion valuation. Index Ventures led. In with them: IVP, Blossom Capital, SAIC, Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Construct Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, NEA, Interlagos, and Bhatt. Use the company's "$2 billion valuation." Call it post-money only in a sentence that is quoting TechCrunch. The machine: one vehicle, upper stage becomes the data center, NVIDIA Space-1 Vera Rubin Modules on the plan, constellation the company calls Stampede. Schedule, once: a first satellite in 2026 to beam power from space to Earth. The rocket year is not one date. Payload: no earlier than the end of 2028. TechCrunch: before the end of 2028. Space.com: in 2028. Say that split once and don't try it again.

3. **How he got here.** Stanford physics, then a master's in math. Met Vlad Tenev at Stanford. Two finance companies in New York. Robinhood in 2013. Co-CEO until November 2020, chief creative officer until March 2024, still a director. Don't print one ownership percentage. Family, then stop: only child, son of two immigrants, father from Bhavnagar in Gujarat, both parents from families in India, a physics PhD at the school he calls "University of Huntsville Alabama," childhood in Poquoson, Virginia, father later "at NASA at Langley Air Force Base." No illness. No "as a South Asian." Then the company before the rename: Aetherflux, public in October 2024, solar power down by infrared laser. April 2025, a $50 million Series A, Index and Interlagos leading, with Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz, and NEA. He told TechCrunch he couldn't buy enough rides, so they're building the rocket. Founding, once: the company says 2024; TechCrunch that October said "late last year."

4. **Landing.** Don't recap. The picture is a $2 billion valuation waiting on one satellite. Does the 2026 power-beaming demo fly? End on that. No moral. No investment advice. Don't rerun the rocket-date split.
