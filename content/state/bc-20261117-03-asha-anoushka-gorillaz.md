## Why now

The Recording Academy nominated Gorillaz' *The Mountain* for Best Alternative Music Album at the 69th Grammy Awards on 16 November 2026. Asha Bhosle sings on one track. Anoushka Shankar plays sitar on six billed tracks and is also credited on a seventh. Indian Grammy-day coverage treated that as a sentence under a Tamil-song story. Bhosle died in Mumbai on 12 April 2026, weeks after the album's 27 February release.
