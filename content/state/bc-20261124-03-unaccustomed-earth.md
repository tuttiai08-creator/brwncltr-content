## The Story

Netflix's Tudum page, published Sept. 17, 2026, says *Unaccustomed Earth* is coming Dec. 17. Hollywood Reporter India, updated Sept. 18, 2026, prints the premiere as December 17, 2026. Madhuri Shekar adapted Jhumpa Lahiri's short-story collection into an eight-episode series about an Indian American Bengali community in Cambridge, Massachusetts, with Freida Pinto as Parul Chaudhury and Siddharth as Amit Mukherjee. Lahiri is an executive producer, and Ritesh Batra directs the first two episodes.

## Why It's Interesting

Shekar told Tudum the stories share a setting, Cambridge, and that the series started from a picture of those characters at one Thanksgiving table. Parul, on that same page, comes back to Cambridge after five years in Mumbai and meets Amit, the love she never quite left. The show is a family drama with a specific city and a specific book, which is a usable read for diaspora millennials who already know *The Namesake*.
