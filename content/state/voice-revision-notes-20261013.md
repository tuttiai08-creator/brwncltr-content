## bc-20260830-01 — Diljit Dosanjh, Wembley

Packet: `content/ready-for-review/20260831-bc-20260830-01-diljit-wembley.md`. Research: `content/research/bc-20260830-01-20260831.md`.

### Proposed lede

Diljit Dosanjh already has Wembley marked sold out on his own tour page. He announced the show from the stage at Toronto's Rogers Centre: "We are doing Wembley Stadium London here. Michael Jackson performed there. Prince performed there."

Canada did the heavy lifting. Billboard Pro puts the Aura World Tour at $45 million and 317,000 tickets, and five Canadian shows sold more tickets than ten in the United States. The U.S. still took more of the money. BC Place in Vancouver opened the run with 42,700 tickets, the biggest attendance of the North American leg. Rogers Centre, on 31 May, grossed $5.9 million, the biggest revenue.

Wembley, on 12 September, is the one stadium on a European leg of arenas. His last London date was the O2 in October 2024. Billboard Canada and Music Business Worldwide both put that room at about 20,000. This is not a UK stadium first. Tottenham Hotspur Stadium's own page calls Arijit Singh the first Indian artist ever to headline a UK stadium, on 5 September 2025. The BBC's Harry Low, that day, called him the first Indian-born artist to headline one.

### Headline

Diljit Sold Out Wembley. Canada Did the Heavy Lifting.

### Dek

Five Canadian shows out-ticketed a longer U.S. run, the U.S. still took more of the money, and the UK stadium first already belongs to Arijit Singh.
