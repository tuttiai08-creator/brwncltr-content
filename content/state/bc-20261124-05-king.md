## The Story

Red Chillies Entertainment dates *King* for 24 December 2026. The same page says Siddharth Anand presents it and directs it, and the cast line names Shah Rukh Khan. Deadline (Nov. 3, 2026) says Phars Film will release the film in international cinemas on December 24, day-and-date with India, where Pen Marudhar has distribution. Red Chillies produces in association with Marflix Pictures. Deadline calls it the reunion of Khan and Anand after *Pathaan* (2023).

## Why It's Interesting

Diaspora audiences meet this film through Phars, not through the India distributor. The date is Christmas Eve, and the international opening is the same day as India. That is a concrete room: whoever is buying a ticket in the US, Canada, or the UK is in Phars's release, on the strength of a Khan-Anand film that follows *Pathaan*.
